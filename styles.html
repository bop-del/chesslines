The header and the language toggle

The app’s name appears on every screen and says the least of anything on it — today it is set larger than the move text. The proposal steps it down and leaves the toggle where it is.

It also drops the “OPENINGS” title, which is a separate choice. Two reasons: it is a tracked-out all-caps label above a heading, which is template chrome rather than information; and once the tabs land, the active tab already says “Openings” a few pixels above it. Keeping the title is possible — this is the one state here that removes something rather than restyling it, so it is called out rather than slipped in.
