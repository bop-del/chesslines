Arrow variants

Seven treatments of the move arrow, each drawn by the real board through the real stylesheet (issue #27). Every variant twice: once on 1.e4, the move it flatters, and once on Nf3, where a straight arrow crosses a square the knight never occupies and so looks its worst. The rings button cycles both → from-ring only → none, which is the ticket's open question. Pick by looking, on the phone. Not part of the app — nothing links here.
